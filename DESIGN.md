# Dok Mai Lao design system
## Overview
The user-approved garden-table direction C combines the unchanged original logo with sunny yellow, hibiscus red and navy. Warm, welcoming and Italian without masking its Pakse identity.
## Colors
Yellow anchors navigation and the visit section. Cream supports menu reading. Navy is the main ink. Red identifies actions, active categories and prices.
## Typography
Display serif expresses the printed menu; Source Sans 3 keeps ingredients, prices and contact details readable. Hero max 86px, mobile 52px. Menu titles 30px desktop, 28px mobile.
## Layout
Panoramic illustrated hero with centered semantic title. Menu lists in two columns, one below 700px. Categories horizontally scroll on small screens. Actual restaurant photos follow the menu. Review and contact/map close the page.
## Elevation & Depth
Flat paper-like colour surfaces. Only buttons gain a soft hover shadow.
## Shapes
Rectangular controls and photographs; circular presentation of the original logo without changing the asset.
## Components
Menu tabs support arrow keys, Home and End. Search spans all categories and has an explicit reset state. Mobile contact navigation remains at the bottom with safe-area padding. Native Google Maps is embedded using the exact place identity, with a direct external fallback.
## Do's and Don'ts
Preserve logo bytes. Keep photo and illustration roles distinct. Never invent opening times, menu prices, reviews or booking confirmations. WhatsApp starts a conversation; it does not confirm a reservation.
